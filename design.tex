  \subsection{Story}\\
  Long ago, when Earth was becoming fully inhospitable, humanity sent out thousands of ships over a period of 300 years, some containing humans, and some containing animals, with the hopes of populating the galaxy. Ships with animals were sent to worlds similar to the habitats that these animals are from. The ice planet of \textbf{Chryomede} became the destination for Earth's arctic species, as it retains a freezing temperature except on the equator, and its surface is largely composed of water.
  
  1.5 million years later, Chryomede is still inhabited by these arctic creatures, though they have undergone significant evolutionary changes. The most significant of these changes being penguins, who have developed an intelligence comparable to that of humans. The protagonist, \textbf{Paavo}, is one of these penguins.

  One day, a ship crash lands onto Chryomede. The human passengers are killed on impact, but the ship is still largely intact. Using information from the ship, Paavo is able to reconstruct the ship from materials on Chryomede, and escape for the first time.

  After leaving Chryomede, Paavo travels all over the galaxy exploring new worlds, trading resources and fighting pirates and bosses. As well as different animal species, different humanoid species have evolved and dominate different regions of the galaxy.

  On these adventures, Paavo finds clues which eventually lead him to the conclusion that a secret, esoteric society of superpowerful beings reside on the beings' home planet, called \textbf{Primorus}, and they are said to be the architects of the galaxy.

  Paavo thus sets on his ultimate quest of finding Primorus. By constructing advanced machinery composed of rare materials from the different worlds, Paavo can analyze different artifacts and reveal information about their origin. Eventually, the location is revealed, and Paavo travels there. 

  Primorus, revealed to be Earth, is completely dominated by beautiful green landscape and billions of creatures. In the middle of the Pacific Ocean lies a temple (The \textbf{Monad}) where a group of immortal jellyfish-like organisms (the \textbf{Mereons}) have been inhabiting for over a million years. In that time, they have dedicated their focus entirely to mental studies and extreme meditation. They are sustained by the other lifeforms on the planet, who treat them as gods. Through pure psychological and spiritual manipulation, they send signals into space which manipulate the interactions and politics of the quadrillions of beings in the galaxy.

  \subsection{Main Aim}
  Learn more about the world

  \subsection{Main Mechanic to Achieve Aim}
  Using vehicles, weapons, tools, exploring different planets and fighting enemies to discover pieces of information. You also collect materials which are used to craft and/or trade in the trading post space stations.

  \begin{multicols}{2}
    \subsection{Enablers}
    \subsubsection{Active}
    Player exploring, fighting, discovering
    \subsubsection{Passive}
    Spaceships crashing, other planned story progress events
    \columnbreak
    \subsection{Blockers}
    \subsubsection{Active}
    Pirates, bosses, mercenaries, other enemies
    \subsubsection{Passive}
    Environment (temperature, atmosphere, weather)
  \end{multicols}
  
  \begin{multicols}{2}
    \subsection {Core Loop}
    \begin{enumerate}
      \item Explore new area (at first new areas of Chryomede, then other planets)
      \item Fight enemies, complete challenges, collect resources/info
      \item Bring resources/info back to home (ship), craft, build, etc.
    \end{enumerate}
    \columnbreak
    \subsection{Win Condition}
      Locate Primorus, find the Monad, and discover the powers of the Mereons.
    \subsection{Lose Conditions(s)}
      Death, though this is a temporary setback.
  \end{multicols}
